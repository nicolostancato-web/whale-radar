# The "34 apps" were mostly one protocol's plumbing — 24 Oct, evening

## Correction to my own claim

I measured 34 distinct fee-collecting addresses and reported "34 applications
doing this today — standard practice, not a trick." That was a deduction, not a
measurement. Named from public sources, most of them are **Zora's own fee
plumbing** (hooks, protocol Safe) and **LI.FI / Jumper**. Both are companies.

**And none of the named entities is a one- or two-person app.** LI.FI was
founded in Berlin in 2021; Jumper is being spun out of it with its own CEO. Of
the four addresses with no name: a Safe, two smart-wallet accounts and an EOA —
a smart-wallet account *can* belong to one person, but there is no public
product behind any of them.

## What the correction revealed, which is better than the claim

The 0.204% median is not an average of assorted rates. **It is one published
line: Zora's platform referral, 0.20% of the trade.** The lower quartile,
0.090%, is two more of their lines added together (0.05% protocol + 0.04% trade
referral).

> **Zora pays 0.20% to whoever brings it the trade**, and it is written in
> their documentation.

The coins that flow carries are **creator and content coins** — the audience is
people who publish, not people who trade.

Published rates alongside it:

| product | fee |
|---|---|
| Zora creator/content coins | 1% pool fee, of which **0.20% is the platform referral** |
| LI.FI | 0.25% service fee |
| Rainbow wallet | 0.85% of trading volume |
| Uniswap apps and API | **0%**, since 28 Dec 2025 |

No published study shows users leaving a Base app over a fee near 0.20%.

## What this changes

The door keeps its price and gains an audience. It is not "build a trading
bot". It is **bring creators and their readers to a platform that pays 0.20%
for having brought them** — which is a distribution problem in a market where
the product already exists and the payment is documented.

And the honest counterweight: **no public record of a solo operator earning
meaningfully from it.** That absence is not proof it cannot be done, but it is
the opposite of encouragement.
