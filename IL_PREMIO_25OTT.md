# The premium: fifth family closed — 25 Oct

The raw dislocation between a perp and its index — distinct from funding, which
is clamped and smoothed. Mechanical reason to revert: if the perp is dear,
arbitrage pulls it back. 112 markets, 346,075 hours.

Calibration exact at all three horizons (both directions average to −0.094%).

| horizon | fade the dislocation | follow it | **gross signal** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4h | −0.0914% ± 0.0262 | −0.0966% ± 0.0262 | +0.003% |
| 8h | −0.1092% ± 0.0446 | −0.0788% ± 0.0446 | +0.015% |
| 24h | −0.1468% ± 0.1066 | −0.0412% ± 0.1066 | **+0.053%** |

**A gross signal exists** — following the dislocation, not fading it — **and it
is smaller than the cost.** Every net result is below zero or not demonstrated.

The rising progression does not impress me: the three horizons share the same
entries, and an hour ago exactly that shape died as soon as the market count
grew. Lesson applied the same day it was learned.

## The invariant across five families closed today

**Gross exists, gross is small, the toll eats it.**

What has changed is only the scale: on memecoins the toll was 6% against a
gross of 1.5%. Here the toll is 0.094% against a gross of 0.05%. Two orders of
magnitude closer — and the direction of the inequality has never once flipped.
