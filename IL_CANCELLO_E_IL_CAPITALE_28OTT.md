# Where skill is paid, the gate is capital — 28 Oct

## The contradiction worth chasing

The convergence said markets pay capital 3-5% and pay skill zero. But one
place in the measured record pays skill: **CoW Protocol solvers, ~EUR 13,000
a day** — the largest piecework pot found, and awarded for solving an
optimisation better than competitors. When "piecework" was closed, the pot
size was noted and the right question never asked: **what can a newcomer
take, and what does it cost to try.**

## The competition is genuinely open

153 consecutive auctions, read from the public API:

| | |
|---|---|
| solvers competing per auction | **18** median, 51 max |
| distinct solvers winning at least once | **17 of 153 auctions** |
| share of the top winner | 28.8% |
| share of the 8th | still 3.3% |
| auctions decided by **under 1%** of score | **27%** |

This is not a monopoly. It is a real contest where marginal skill decides a
quarter of the outcomes — the profile of a trade that pays ability.

## And the entrance ticket is six figures

To compete you need a bonding pool:

| | required |
|---|---|
| full pool | **$500,000** in yield-bearing stables + **1,500,000 COW** |
| reduced pool (CIP-44, explicitly created to lower the barrier) | **$50,000** + **500,000 COW**, rising to $100,000 + 1,000,000 COW within a year |

Sources: [CIP-44](https://forum.cow.fi/t/cip-44-reduced-bonding-requirements/2424),
[bonding pools](https://docs.cow.fi/cow-protocol/reference/core/auctions/bonding-pools).

The only non-capital path is being vouched by the CoW DAO's own pool, which
is discretionary, limited, and not something that can be measured or planned
for.

## What it adds to the picture

Capital is not only what gets **paid**. It is also the **gate**.

> Where skill is paid, money is required to enter. Where no money is required
> to enter, nothing is paid.

That is the same wall seen from the other side, and it is a more complete
statement than yesterday's. Fifteen families now say it.
