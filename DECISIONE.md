# The decision, on one page — 26 Oct 2026

Three months. **EUR 52.16 unchanged. Nothing earned, nothing lost, EUR 0.26
spent** on advice. What follows is everything needed to choose, with the
numbers measured rather than assumed.

## The law we found

> **A fee is the price of a risk.** Any income stream pays for something borne:
> market risk, capital tied up, or work that is hard to replicate. Bear
> nothing, and anyone can do it — so it is competed to zero.

Ten closed families were all attempts to be paid **without bearing anything**,
wearing ten different predictors. That is why each one died the same way:
**the gross exists, the gross is small, the toll eats it.**

## Update — 27 Oct, evening: option 3 no longer stands

Option 3 (build the product that collects a routing fee) rested on a measured
share of swaps paying a cut to a third address. Two corrections, both today:

1. The share was **divided by seven** — withdrawals had been counted as
   transactions (6.7 withdrawals per paying transaction). 5%, not 47-68%.
2. Worse, those cuts are **not app fees at all**. 98% of recipients collect on
   a single token; one token pays 55 distinct recipients. They are token
   transfer taxes and holder distributions — something you issue, not
   something you build. Exactly one address in the sample behaves like a real
   routing app.

Details in `L_ULTIMA_ARCHITETTURA_CHIUSA_27OTT.md`.

**So the choice is now between two options, not three**, and all seven
economic architectures are closed with numbers. The map is complete, and it
says what is being looked for is not inside the space that was searched.
