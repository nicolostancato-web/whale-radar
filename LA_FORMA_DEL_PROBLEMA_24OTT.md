# The shape of the problem — 24 Oct, morning

Five roads closed in one night, thirteen in three months, and every one died of
the same two causes: either the gross did not cover the toll, or **the entire
pie was EUR 40–94 a day**. When a cause repeats that often it stops being a
property of the strategies and becomes a property of the environment.

So this morning, instead of road fourteen, the ceiling was measured.

## The biggest pie available, measured

Uniswap v3 on Base, the largest venue we can reach:

- **~EUR 166M daily volume**, **~EUR 74,000 a day in fees paid by traders**
- Across the 40 busiest pools: **EUR 51M of capital earning EUR 32,390 a day**
- Gross yield: **23.2% a year**

A good yield. But it is distributed **in proportion to capital deposited**, so
on EUR 52 it is **EUR 12 a year** — before impermanent loss, which on those
pairs is real.

## The exception that closes the circle

One pool in the table holds EUR 24,830 and collects EUR 110 of fees in thirty
minutes: a notional **7,769% a year**. That is exactly the kind of pool where
yield per euro is enormous — and exactly the kind where we measured last night
that **the price loss eats all of it** (−2.9% ± 0.4 per two-minute position,
concentrated).

High yield exists only where the risk that cancels it lives. That is not a
coincidence; it is the market price of that risk.

## The structural conclusion, in one sentence

**Every income stream on these chains is distributed either in proportion to
capital, or to speed. We have neither.**

Thirteen roads said it thirteen different ways:

| limited by | roads |
|---|---|
| the toll exceeding the gross | whales, copying, graduation arbitrage, cross-pool arbitrage, the 2x rule, the merchant |
| the pie being tiny | UniswapX (EUR 40/day), liquidations (EUR 94/day) |
| our capital | LP on Base (EUR 12/year on EUR 52) |
| the door being shut by construction | Jupiter Lock gifts, equities (off-AMM) |

## What this changes

This is not a surrender — it is the first time we have the **shape** of the
problem rather than another failed attempt. It says the next move is not a
fourteenth strategy on these chains. It is a place where **our size is not the
binding constraint**.
