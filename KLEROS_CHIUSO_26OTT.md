# Kleros: alive, and twenty-one euros a day for the whole protocol — 26 Oct

Second of the three survivors of the cost screen. It survived because a juror
pays no toll: you are not buying anything, you are supplying a judgement.

## Finding the live court

The Ethereum contract `0x988b3A53…` **exists** (23,207 bytes) and emitted
**zero events in 30 days**. That alone distinguishes nothing — two days ago
Morpho was the mirror case, absent at its canonical address and thriving
elsewhere. Grok confirmed the live court is **V2 on Arbitrum**.

My own first scan of Arbitrum returned zero — because I used **200,000-block
steps**, which the RPC rejects, and counted the rejections as zeros. The same
silent-failure class, for the third time. Rescanned with 9,000-block steps,
116 windows, **zero failures**.

## The measurement

Kleros V2 on Arbitrum, `0x991d2df1…`, over three days:

| event | per day |
|---|---|
| **jurors drawn** | **2** |
| disputes created | 1 |
| rulings | 3 |

At the published EUR 10.46 per coherent vote, **the entire protocol pays about
EUR 21 a day** to every juror in the world combined. Winning *every* draw —
impossible, they are random among stakers — would be EUR 21.

With roughly fifty staked jurors competing, our share would be about **EUR 0.40
a day**.

## Same wall, third form

UniswapX: EUR 40/day for a whole chain. Morpho liquidations: EUR 94/day.
Kleros: EUR 21/day. **Not a question of skill — the pot does not exist.**

Closed on capacity, in twenty minutes, without testing a single strategy.
