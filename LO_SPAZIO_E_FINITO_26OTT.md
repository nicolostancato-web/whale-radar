# The space of architectures is finite, and we have walked all of it — 26 Oct

The law: **income pays whoever bears something.** There are only so many things
one can bear. Enumerated, with what we measured for each:

| what you bear | what it pays | measured | verdict for EUR 52 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a forecast being right** | the move, minus the toll | ten families, gross < toll every time | **closed** — and Astra named why: we kept changing the predictor, never the architecture |
| **market risk, as a liquidity supplier** | a contractual fee | memecoin LP 1% fee but ruinous divergence; stable LP 0.001% fee | **closed by the law itself**: the fee *is* the price of the risk, so no-risk means no fee |
| **supplying the missing side** (funding harvest) | hourly funding | −0.92%/month on the accessible direction; the paying direction needs a spot borrow | **closed** — accessible side loses, paying side inaccessible |
| **patience / locked capital** | interest, proportional to the lock | T-bills 4–5%/yr, HLP 3.7%/yr, Base pools 23% gross | **arithmetically closed**: EUR 2–12 a *year* on EUR 52 |
| **operational burden, no customers** | keeper rewards | the reward sits just above gas and several keepers show up | **closed** — it is a speed race |
| **operational burden, with customers** | an app fee written in the transaction | 0.295% median, EUR 169,000/day of volume for EUR 500/day | **open — the only one** |
| **adjudication / piecework** | a fixed fee per job | Kleros EUR 21/day worldwide; UMA needs a USD 750 bond | **closed** on capacity and on capital |

## What this means

This is not "another family failed". It is that **the categories are
exhaustive**: one is paid for being right, for bearing risk, for locking
capital, for speed, or for doing work someone wants. We have now put a measured
number on every one of them from this starting position.

**Six are closed. One is open.** The open one is the only category where what
you bear is *work that is hard to replicate* rather than risk, capital or
latency — which is exactly why it does not get competed to zero.

Continuing to search inside the closed six is not pessimism to avoid; it is
arithmetic already done.
