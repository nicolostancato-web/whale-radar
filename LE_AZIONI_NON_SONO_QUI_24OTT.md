# Tenth road closed: the equities are real, the market is not here — 24 Oct

Robinhood Chain does carry genuine tokenized equities. Read from the contracts
themselves:

- `NVDA` → **"NVIDIA • Robinhood Token"**
- `SPCX` → **"Space Exploration Technologies Corp. Class A Common Stock •
  Robinhood Token"** — SpaceX, a private company

Grok's idea was to provide liquidity overnight, when the US cash market is shut
and a buyer pays a wide spread because nobody can hedge. The idea rests on a
clock rather than on a pattern, which is why it was worth testing first.

**It fails on a fact: there is no AMM market for them.** Over the last 10.3
hours, of **917 pools** trading against USDG or ETH with at least three swaps,
**one** is a real equity. The pools that pair an equity with a memecoin
(NVDA/ALP, TSLA/VAJRA, SPCX/BLACKHOLE) are dead — single digits of swaps.

The equity liquidity lives inside Robinhood's own venue, where we cannot post.

## Noted in passing, as the eleventh road

**`AAPLx3L/USDG`, 3,062 swaps** — a 3× leveraged Apple token. Leveraged products
rebalance daily and decay structurally, regardless of where the underlying goes.
That is another mechanism rather than a pattern. Parked, not chased.
