## 1. What counts—and what the numbers would mean

There are three materially different things under “permissionless on-chain markets”:

1. **On-chain positions:** derivatives, outcome shares, NFTs, claims.
2. **On-chain settlement of an external service:** compute, storage, bandwidth. The service itself is not executed on-chain.
3. **Open protocol jobs:** submitting proofs, reporting outcomes, maintaining protocol state.

An open buyer interface does **not** imply an open supplier interface. Nor does a transferable token imply unrestricted redemption of its underlying asset.

For capacity, the useful quantities differ:

| Market | Relevant capacity measurement |
|---|---|
| Trading | Executable depth, spreads, turnover, transaction costs—not simply volume |
| Paid services | **Customer-funded payments to outside suppliers**, excluding deposits, refunds and token subsidies |
| Oracle/proof/keeper jobs | Rewards actually paid, eligible operators and job frequency |
| RWA claims | Unrestricted secondary turnover and redemption eligibility |

In the tables below, **NE = current daily volume or supplier fee pool in euros not established from available evidence.** It does not mean zero.

The “killer” column identifies the likely constraint for *your profile*, not a demonstrated failure based on measurements I have not performed.
