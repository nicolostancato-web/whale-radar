# My own hypothesis, tested properly, dead — 26 Oct

**The idea:** the commission is identical on every Binance perp (0.05% a leg),
but the competition is not. So the lower tiers should keep an inefficiency the
top ones have lost. Mechanical reason, free data, passes every filter.

**First test, two tiers:** large markets −0.027% gross, markets 300-380
**+0.041%**. Sign flips. I reported that my structural prediction was
confirmed.

**It was two points.**

## Five tiers

| tier | gross signal |
|---|---|
| 1-80 (largest) | −0.027% |
| 150-230 | **−0.066%** |
| 300-380 | +0.041% |
| 450-530 | +0.045% |
| 600-680 | **−0.028%** |

Not an ordered decline: positive in the middle, negative at both ends. Tier
150-230 is **more negative than the largest markets** — the opposite of the
hypothesis. With intervals of ±0.065 these are all compatible with each other
and with noise around −0.04%.

**Hypothesis not confirmed. Withdrawn.**

Same error as the funding horizons two days ago: two values that rise look like
a trend and are not. **Two points do not make a line** — and the fix is always
the same, add points before claiming.

Calibration was exact in all five tiers (−0.1000%), so the measurement is sound
and the "no" is reliable.

**Cost: two hours instead of two weeks.** It leaves three quarters of a million
hours of data on small markets that we did not have.
