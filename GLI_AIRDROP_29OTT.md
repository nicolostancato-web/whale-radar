# Airdrops — the eighteenth family, closed in one round — 29 Oct

The last crypto road that is **capital-light and work-heavy**: protocols pay
people for using them, and the payout scales with effort rather than with
money deposited — the opposite of everything else measured here.

## The numbers that close it

From published 2026 data: 51 airdrops across Ethereum, BNB, Base and
Arbitrum, each paying at least 5,000 wallets and $1M, totalling 2.02M wallets
and $809M at claim-day prices.

| | |
|---|---|
| what the **median claimer** received | **$50** |
| gas cost of farming | **$10–100 per wallet per month** |
| over six months of activity | **~$2,000** |
| airdrops with automated anti-sybil filters | **85%** |

**Fifty dollars of revenue against two thousand of cost.** Not marginal —
deeply negative. And the filters specifically disqualify the one response
that would scale it: running many wallets. In the median drop 11% of
claimers were in linked groups and were filtered.
