# The toll's signature, seen four times — 27 Oct

## Thirteenth closure: the carry ranking

Buy the coins that **pay** funding, sell the coins that **cost** it, within
one venue. A ranking, so the common tide cancels. Seven years of Binance
funding, 39 coins, 6,902 instants with at least 20 coins present. Independent
unit: the date.

| hold | cycles | net |
|---|---|---|
| 8h | 1,988 | **−0.1894% ± 0.0418** |
| 24h | 662 | **−0.1928% ± 0.1295** |
| 72h | 220 | +0.0547% ± 0.4005 |
| 168h | 94 | −0.2128% ± 1.1852 |
| 504h | 31 | +1.6109% ± 2.8950 |

The cost is **0.20%**. The net at 8 and 24 hours is −0.189% and −0.193%.
Gross is zero. Longer horizons are fog.

## The regularity worth naming

This exact shape appeared **four times today, on independent families**:

| family | cost | net measured |
|---|---|---|
| cross-venue arbitrage (realistic entry) | 0.19% | −0.190%, −0.195%, −0.171% |
| price ranking across markets, 12-24h | 0.20% | −0.196%, −0.269%, −0.208% |
| carry ranking, 8-24h | 0.20% | −0.189%, −0.193% |
| volume spike, held-out markets | 0.10% | −0.063%, −0.179%, −0.159% |

Every time, the net lands on the toll to the second decimal. That is not
repeated bad luck — it is the **fingerprint of a market where the gross has
been competed away to zero**, and four independent confirmations show it more
convincingly than any single one.

It is also the empirical content of the frontier: the ceiling is not a
judgement about our skill, it is a measured property of these venues. Where
money flows, what is left after the toll is zero, repeatedly and precisely.

## A tempting corollary, checked and dead

From the signature a natural idea follows: the gross is competed down to the
toll **of the competitors**, not ours. At €52 our slippage is 0.006%; someone
moving €1m pays 0.13%. If the equilibrium is set by the large players, there
should be room underneath for a small one.

**The data says no.** What was measured is not "zero after a large player's
cost" but **zero outright**:

| family | cost charged | net | implied gross |
|---|---|---|---|
| price ranking, 12h | 0.20% | −0.1963% | **+0.004%** |
| carry ranking, 8h | 0.20% | −0.1894% | **+0.011%** |
| cross-venue arb, 1h delay | 0.19% | −0.1901% | **−0.000%** |

And the held-out volume-spike test showed **no size gradient** across market
tiers (+0.088% / −0.029% / +0.036% from tier 150 to tier 600) — if small
markets left room for small players, it would appear there first.

Written down because this idea will occur again, to me or to a later session,
and it costs half a day to rediscover that it is already dead.
