# Everything real pays capital, never skill — 27 Oct

## The fourteenth family: the volatility premium

Options price uncertainty higher than the uncertainty that arrives. Four
years of Deribit's implied-volatility index against the volatility actually
realised in the following 30 days, **50 independent non-overlapping windows**
per asset:

| | implied | realised | premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 51.33% | 44.86% | **+6.47 ± 3.26** vol points, 40/50 windows |
| ETH | 64.07% | 59.51% | +4.56 ± 4.30, 34/50 |

Real, and it survives the checks. (BTC and ETH move together, so these are
two agreeing readings, not two independent samples — today's lesson.)

Converted into money, as a variance position:

| | per 30-day window | annualised | worst window |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | +0.401% ± 0.254 | **+4.8%** | −3.0% |
| ETH | +0.224% ± 0.338 | +2.7%, not demonstrated | −3.4% |

Before option spreads, which are far wider than on perpetuals, and before the
fact that 52 euro cannot open a Deribit position at all.

## The convergence, which matters more than the result

Everything this project has found that is **real** pays the same number:

| | annual |
|---|---|
| cross-venue funding spread | **+3.2%** |
| volatility premium on BTC | **+4.8%** |

That is not a coincidence. Both are payment for **supplying capital** —
lending, and insuring. The market pays whoever puts money up, reliably, at
about 3-5% a year.

**It does not pay for being right.** That was measured at zero thirteen times
today, and the net landed on the toll to the second decimal four times.

## The sharpest statement of the problem so far

> Every true thing we find is compensation for capital, and capital is
> exactly what we do not have.

52 euro at 5% is **2.60 euro a year**. The search has not failed to find
edges; it has found that the only durable edges in this space are interest
rates, and an interest rate on nothing is nothing.

This reframes the question Nicolo has to answer. It is not "which strategy" —
it is whether the next effort goes into finding an edge that pays skill
(thirteen roads say no) or into getting capital, after which the measurements
already done say exactly what it would earn.
