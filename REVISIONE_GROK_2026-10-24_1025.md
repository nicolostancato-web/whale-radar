The wall you measured is an economic identity, not a list of bad trades. I am going to separate three things your results already imply: where the payer is not paying for capital or speed, which market structures you never opened, and the capital level at which each closed road could pay EUR 500/day. Then the question of whether the search itself is the mistake.The wall is real, and it is not an artifact of the fifteen roads. A public on-chain market pays a durable EUR 500 a day to capital, to speed, to a scarce skill that is a different job, or to a franchise. EUR 52 and one second of latency supply none of those. The roads you have not opened use the same four payers.

EUR 500 a day is EUR 182,500 a year. On EUR 52 that is 962% a day. Nothing in the structures below pays that as a yield. The only coherent reading of the target is a machine that compounds until it produces EUR 500 a day. The arithmetic of that reading is at the end.

## 1. Where the discriminator is neither capital nor speed

There are four categories. In each, the payer and the reason they cannot stop paying are different from the AMM, the lending pool, and the intent auction you already measured.

**A treasury buying a behavior it cannot yet get for free.** Points, airdrops, liquidity-reward budgets, grants. The payer is the token holder, through dilution or a treasury. They pay because a new market has no organic users and cannot compel them. The discriminator is matching the recruitment rule — identity, attention, a prescribed action — during the window in which the treasury has overestimated how many people will show up. Equilibrium is the subsidy equaling the cost of performing the behavior. Profit is only the miscalculation. The payer's entire purpose is to shrink that miscalculation to zero, which is the state you already measured on Jupiter Lock: receipts that were a carousel and netted to zero once the set of addresses was accounted for. Polymarket's current perps program distributes $75,000 a day and looks like a counterexample. It is the same category in its competed form. Eligibility is 1% of trailing seven-day maker volume, the pool is split by score, and the score rewards quotes tight to the midpoint. Tight quotes are the trade your concentrated-liquidity result already priced at −1.8% to −2.9%.

**A correct answer on a horizon where arrival time is not the constraint.** Someone pays you because you are right and they are constrained: a fund redeeming, a treasury executing a written policy, a vest unlocking, a hedger who needs the hedge more than the last tick. The discriminator is forecast quality. This is the only trading category that sits outside the wall conceptually. It dies the moment it becomes a rule you can leave running. A public slow rule is a race with a delay, and someone at a lower latency takes the forced flow inside the second you are waiting. What remains is judgment that cannot be reduced to a calibrated rule. Your own copy-trading measurement is this category with the numbers filled in: three wallets chosen after they had won were +35%; fifty-eight wallets chosen in advance by the criterion were −38.3%. The discriminator was the hindsight, not the criterion.

**Work that is judged on correctness rather than arrival.** Bug bounties, audit contests, oracle disputes scored on the true outcome, grants paid for software someone runs. The payer is a protocol that loses more by not paying, because an exploit costs more than the bounty and a bad oracle corrupts every market that reads it. The discriminator is the quality of the work. This is the one category in which some people clear EUR 500 a day. The EUR 52 does not appear in the equation. Nothing compounds. The income is wages for a skill. A person who does not already have the skill is outside the category in the same way they are outside any other profession.

**A fee for trust on capital you do not own.** Management and performance fees. The payer is an owner who will not operate the position themselves, and they pay because their time is worth more than the fee. The discriminator is a track record someone will fund. This is the only arithmetic route from a small personal bankroll to EUR 500 a day that does not require a fantasy return: at a 1% fee the book has to be EUR 18.3 million of someone else's money. The record you have, run through the instrument you trust, is a record of closed roads. That record does not raise money.

No fifth category appears once you leave spot AMMs.

## 2. The structures you have not touched are the same wall

You examined AMM spot, one lending protocol's liquidations, and one intent market. The structures outside that set, as of this week:

**Perpetual futures.** Hyperliquid's protocol vault, HLP, holds about $177 million and has returned 3.7% annualized recently, 4.1% over the last 93 days. Funding is a transfer proportional to notional. Liquidations on that venue are internalized by the vault's own backstop, so the pie you measured from the outside on Morpho is, on the largest perp exchange, already captured by a pool you can only join in proportion to capital. EUR 52 earns about EUR 2 a year there.

**Options.** The premium is payment for gap risk. The discriminator is capital, and the loss you have not yet sampled is the gap.

**Prediction markets.** Event-market maker rebates are running around $165,000 a day in October, paid across roughly 159,000 wallets, pro-rata to people whose resting quotes get filled. The top of that distribution is a professional quoting operation. The quoting is adverse selection with a subsidy attached. You have already measured the adverse selection.

**Intent solvers other than the UniswapX market you sized.** CoW paid solvers about $3.6 million in 2026 through mid-September, on the order of $14,000 a day, 52% to one team, against roughly $1 million of gas. The fill is usually close to the public market. The whole visible subsidy for that profession is a few tens of thousands of dollars a day before engineering, inventory, and losing days. Your Base UniswapX figure of EUR 40 a day for the entire chain is the same structure on a smaller venue, not a small venue's anomaly.

**Bridge relaying.** Across relayers, in a recent 30-day window, earned on the order of $300,000 of fees between them, about $10,000 a day, at 2 to 5 basis points. Three addresses did about 80% of the volume. The top address earned about $3,000 a day on roughly $470 million of monthly volume. They earn it by having inventory already on the destination chain and by winning the quote. Inventory is capital. The quote is speed. In July 2026 a flaw in relayer infrastructure took about $4.5 million out of relayer wallets. The residual risk is sized for hot wallets holding millions.

**Cash-and-carry basis** between a perp and a spot or a staking rate. Still capital. A generous sustained 30% a year, which is a mania print rather than a machine, needs about EUR 600,000 to produce EUR 500 a day. A normal single-digit funding rate needs several million.

**Bribe and governance markets.** The payer is buying voting power. Voting power is capital or delegated trust.

**Tokenized treasuries.** Four to five percent, strictly capital, worse than the 23.2% gross you already measured on the busy pools.

**Primary issuance.** You measured the public form: every graduated coin, bought at minute one, −5.0% ± 2.4 over 5,139 coins. The private form is an allocation list. That is a relationship, which you do not have.

Quieter chains do not escape this. A chain with less competition has less flow. You already saw the mature form of that result: the entire Base UniswapX pie is EUR 40 a day, and the entire Base small-debt liquidation pie is EUR 1 a day.

## 4. The reading the evidence supports

Public on-chain trading does not produce EUR 500 a day from EUR 52 for a participant without capital, without speed, and without a scarce skill or a franchise. Continuing the search across public flow is the error.

The reason is the size of the target. A reliable EUR 180,000 a year, extracted from a market anyone can enter, is worth a skilled year of someone's life. Those people are already in the auction. They bid until the residual equals the cost of being them. What is left for a late arrival at EUR 52 and one second is exactly the menu you measured: a capital yield of EUR 12 a year, a speed auction you cannot enter, or a gross that does not clear the toll.

Your instrument is good enough that this is not a problem of sample size. It saw −5% on 5,139 coins and −38% on 58 wallets. An edge large enough to matter at EUR 52 would be large enough for that instrument to have caught it. The edges small enough to hide in the noise do not reach the target inside a planning horizon:

| A true daily edge, after tolls | Capital that earns EUR 500/day | Time to compound EUR 52 to that capital, with no losing days |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10% a day, about 44% a year, a strong systematic result | EUR 500,000 | about 25 years |
| 1% a day | EUR 50,000 | about 690 days |
| 2% a day | EUR 25,000 | about 310 days |

A 1% daily edge that survived for two years would be a famous result, and it would attract the capital and the speed that close it. Leverage does not connect these rows to 962% a day. A real edge of that size is bet as a fraction of the bankroll. Levering it toward the target is how a −38% criterion becomes a total loss in a day. Levering the results you actually have has the same outcome, faster.

The evidence that would overturn this is one object. A rule written down before any of the trades, with the toll inside the accounting, on a market named in advance. A confidence interval on daily profit whose lower bound is positive. Then a second sample, collected after the rule was frozen, that still clears. Then a size test that raises the ticket until the edge bends, and shows the bent curve still producing EUR 500 a day at a size you can fund, in a window that contains the strategy's losing regime. One number, pre-registered, out of sample, with capacity.

Another screen of wallets that already won, another subsidy that paid someone once, another backtest before gas, or another chain with the same AMM, would be the same evidence you already have.
