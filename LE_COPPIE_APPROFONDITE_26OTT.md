# Pairs, deepened: a real effect worth a fifth of what it costs — 26 Oct

Nicolò asked for a shallow closure to be made deep. Family 5 had been closed on
**ten hand-picked pairs**. Here are **all 435** pairs from 30 Binance markets,
each run against the identical signals at shuffled times.

## The first family whose centre actually moves

| | median t | positive | extremes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **real pairs** | **+0.47** | **281 of 435** | 59 |
| shuffled | +0.00 | ~217 expected | 20 |

In price patterns and attention the centres **coincided** and only the tails
widened — the signature of noise. Here the centre is **displaced**. Taken at
face value the sign count is six standard deviations; not taken at face value,
because 30 markets generate 435 pairs and each market appears in 29 of them, so
the effective independent count is perhaps thirty.

Still: pairs of markets **do revert toward their usual ratio**. It is a diffuse
property, not a lucky tail.

## And it is too small to trade

| | |
|---|---|
| gross per operation | **+0.0203% ± 0.0195** (158,224 non-overlapping ops) |
| cost, crossing the spread | 0.100% |
| cost, posting (with the measured maker advantage) | 0.089% |
| **net** | **−0.069%** |

One trap avoided: the **median pair** returns +0.082%, four times the pooled
mean, which looks much better. It is an artefact — pairs with many operations
have smaller returns and dominate the pooled average. And even +0.082% sits
**below** the 0.089% cost.

## Status

**Closure upgraded from shallow to definitive**, and this time we know what was
inside: a genuine effect, one fifth the size of the toll.

Tenth time the same shape — real, small, eaten. But the first time the centre
moved at all, which is worth recording: the market does revert; it just does
not revert enough to pay the ferryman.
